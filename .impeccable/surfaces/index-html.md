# Surface brief: index.html (home, the whole site)

Mode: Persuade (recruiter decides to read the CV / reach out). Audience, job and evidence: see PRODUCT.md.
Decision channel: worker run, no owner interview; directions inferred from the brief, roll honoured (assigned grounded candidate 5).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a conversation that answers a recruiter's questions in the order they ask them (who, when, CV, what have you shipped, how to reach you). It refuses the category default of hero-headline + card grid + intro animation.

OWN-WORLD: A messaging thread that is ours, not any app's: cool grey ground (near-black in dark), white "visitor question" bubbles on the right, saffron (#FFC21A family) answer bubbles on the left with ink text, document bubble for the CV, album bubbles for screenshots, centred chips as section headings, a sticky composer bar whose quick-reply chips are the navigation. Archivo (variable width) self-hosted: expanded heavy for the name, normal width for text; tabular figures for every number.

STORY: In one screen the visitor knows name, pitch, availability (25 Jan 2027, min 12 weeks) and has the CV one tap away; scrolling, they see two live projects with outcomes first, then experience with numbers, then education, then contact.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone: chat header (monogram avatar, name, status "Available from 25 Jan 2027"), then the contact card (expanded name, pitch, 3 proof chips, Email / LinkedIn / GitHub), the first answer bubble and the CV document bubble with Download; composer bar pinned at the bottom with quick replies and a CV button. Desktop >= 1024: left sticky contact panel (same content), thread on the right.

FORM: candidate 5 of 7 (messaging thread). seed:1e6b311defd4118f. Raises: from VU-meter bridge, one identical module anatomy for every project (outcome, proof, stack) so the reader compares by sweeping; from cassette deck, every control names its action and target (Download CV, PDF); from rain-night city, real dates as timestamps carry chronology; from ink basin, the newest/strongest fact gets the darkest weight. Signature interaction: quick-reply chip jumps to its answer and the answer bubble flashes (jump-to-message highlight).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
